BUS 140 Course Reflection

Student: Ben Thomo

Date: September 10, 2025

My BUS 140 Learning Journey

What I Learned

BUS 140 has been an incredible journey into the fundamentals of business and entrepreneurship. Throughout this course, I gained essential knowledge that directly contributed to developing my business concept for CyberShield Solutions, an IT security company for small businesses.

Key areas of learning included:

  • Business Plan Development: Learning how to create comprehensive business plans with market analysis, financial projections, and competitive assessments
  • Market Research: Understanding target markets and identifying gaps in the marketplace like the cybersecurity protection gap for small businesses
  • Financial Planning: Creating realistic revenue projections and understanding investment requirements
  • Marketing Strategies: Developing value propositions and understanding how to position products in competitive markets
  • Operations Management: Planning business operations, including technology infrastructure and service delivery models

Business Application: CyberShield Solutions

The course directly influenced my development of CyberShield Solutions, which addresses the critical need for affordable cybersecurity protection among small businesses. Using BUS 140 principles, I identified that 60% of small businesses close within 6 months of a cyber attack, yet most can't afford enterprise-level security solutions.

Our business model offers three service tiers ranging from $99-599/month, targeting the 10,000+ small businesses in the Metro Business District with 24/7 monitoring and support.

Course Highlights

My favorite aspects of BUS 140 included:

  1. Real World Case Studies: Analyzing actual business scenarios helped bridge theory with practical application
  2. Financial Modeling Exercises: Learning to create 5-year revenue projections (like forecasting growth from $500,000 to $6.8 million)
  3. Market Competition Analysis: Understanding how to position against established competitors while finding market gaps
  4. Presentation Skills: Developing professional business pitch capabilities

Areas for Improvement

While the course was comprehensive, I wish we had covered more about:

  • Digital Marketing: More focus on online marketing strategies and social media business applications
  • Technology Integration: Deeper exploration of how technology disrupts traditional business models
  • International Business: Global market considerations and cross-border business operations
  • Sustainability: How to build environmentally responsible business practices from the start

Challenges and Growth

My least favorite aspect was initially the financial calculations and projections. The mathematical complexity of creating realistic business models felt overwhelming at first. However, this challenge became one of my greatest learning experiences as I developed confidence in financial analysis.

Image Selection Rationale

I chose the CyberShield logo because it represents the culmination of my learning journey in BUS 140. This professional brand identity emerged directly from applying course principles to create a comprehensive cybersecurity business solution for small businesses. The shield symbol with integrated circuit patterns perfectly communicates the fusion of security and technology that defines modern business protection. The logo embodies the entrepreneurial mindset and business planning skills that BUS 140 taught me, transforming an abstract business concept into a concrete visual identity ready for the marketplace.

Personal Reflections

BUS 140 transformed my understanding of entrepreneurship from abstract concepts to actionable strategies. The course helped me realize that successful businesses don't just solve problems they solve problems that people are willing to pay to have solved, at a price point that makes business sense.

The skills I've gained will be essential as I continue developing CyberShield Solutions. Understanding market positioning, financial planning, and operational scaling will help me turn this business concept into a viable company that can genuinely help small businesses protect themselves in our increasingly digital world.

Overall Course Rating

I would rate this course 9.5/10 for its practical relevance, engaging content, and direct applicability to real world business development.
